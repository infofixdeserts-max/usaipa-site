The rule of levels

A context for a higher level can only be registered when every lower level under it has (a) a registered context and (b) recorded agreement to send elected representatives to the higher table. If even one lower level is missing or objects, the higher level stays locked. No exceptions.

Example: a state context requires all counties in that state registered and agreeing. A county context requires its cities and towns. A city or town context is the foundation - it needs only its own people.
