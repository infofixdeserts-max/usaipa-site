Register a community context

A context is a declaration of how people living in a community want their lives to be: social, economic, and environmental, near term and hundreds of years out. It is created by consensus, includes everyone with veto power, and contains no decisions, no prejudice, and nothing quantified. Higher levels require every lower level to exist and agree.
