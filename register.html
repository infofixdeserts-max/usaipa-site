Register a Context

Put your community's word on the public record.

The rule of levels. A context for a higher level can only be registered when every lower level under it has (a) a registered context and (b) recorded agreement to send elected representatives to the higher table. If even one lower level is missing or objects, the higher level stays locked. No exceptions.

Example: a state context requires all counties in that state registered and agreeing. A county context requires its cities and towns. A city or town context is the foundation - it needs only its own people.

Start a registration






How filings are reviewed

A registration is a public act, not a database entry. Filings are submitted by email to the registry stewards and, once levels check out, appended to the public registry file on this site through an auditable pull request anyone can read. The hierarchy lock is checked by hand and by code before anything goes live. Nothing is auto-approved.

What you need before filing: a context written by consensus (no decisions, no rankings), a list of the people who wrote it, and for every level above a town, the registry links of each lower level under you showing registered status and recorded agreement.
