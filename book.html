The Book

A Third Foundation for the Discarded Half - 80 pages, proposal and field manual, fifty-state ballot access annex.

The Independent Parties of America is a proposal and a field manual. Half of America has no voice, not because it is silent but because it was never offered a sentence worth signing. This book is that sentence: written by communities in their own words, tested by seven honest questions, protected by fail-safe clauses, paid for by tithes instead of masters, and bounded always by the Constitution and the Bill of Rights.

The path runs through folding chairs, petition clipboards, and fifty state rulebooks that yield to anyone patient enough to read them. No permission is required. No permission was ever required.
