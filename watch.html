Liberty County

Three films on how it actually starts

Liberty County is made up, and every county has a version of it. These three films show the loop in order: three neighbours decide to start, fifty people write the context, and the finished document goes onto the public record where anyone can read it.

Total run time 2 minutes 37 seconds. These films carry no narrator: the words on screen are the whole script, and each one has a downloadable transcript below its player.

01
Film one · 45 seconds

Three People and a County

Three neighbours decide the county should speak for itself. They write fifty names, and the list is built to include the people who voted against them.

0:45 720p Captions burned in Download transcript (SRT)
The point of film one. A county is small enough that people can know each other and big enough to matter. If the room only holds people who already agree, it is a club, not a county.
02
Film two · 54 seconds

The Holistic Context

Fifty people fill a gymnasium and write the county's context: the language rule that lets every faith sign, timber as the backbone of the economy, and the five hundred year picture.

0:54 720p Captions burned in Download transcript (SRT)
The language rule. The context cannot say we are a Christian community or a Jewish community, because that decision was already made by somebody and it is not theirs to make. So the line reads: all religions are supported and encouraged in our community. Every faith gets the same sentence.
03
Film three · 58 seconds

Put It On The Record

A screen recording of the real registration flow on usaipa.org. Two towns file first, then the county, and the public registry fills in as each one lands.

0:58 720p Captions burned in Download transcript (SRT)
Nothing is auto approved. A filing joins the public registry only after steward review, through a pull request anyone can read. A county context cannot jump over its own towns.

Your county is not Liberty County

The films use an invented county so that no real place is spoken for. The process is the same everywhere, and it starts with a town, not a county. Write the context, gather the signatures, file it, then the next level up unlocks.
