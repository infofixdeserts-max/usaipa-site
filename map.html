How the levels connect

A higher-level context can only exist when every lower level under it has both (a) registered its own context and (b) agreed to send elected representatives to write the higher one. A missing lower level locks the higher one. Power flows upward as language, not as money.

  • National context - requires all 50 state contexts registered and in agreement
  • State context - requires all county contexts registered and in agreement
  • County context - requires the city and town contexts inside it
  • City or town context - the foundation; requires only its own people
