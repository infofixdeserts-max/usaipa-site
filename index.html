How it works

1. Write the context

Your community answers three questions in its own words: how you want to live, what has to be produced to live that way, and what the land and water must look like hundreds of years from now.

2. Register it here

Post your town, county, state, or national context at usaipa.org. Every context is public, dated, and signed by the people who wrote it. Lower levels must exist and agree before a higher level may be written.

3. Candidates prove authorship

To run on the IPA ticket you must have helped write your community's context. No wealth, no family name, no famous backers. Authorship is the only gate.

4. Seven questions

Every policy a candidate creates or votes on is tested in order: cause and effect, weak link, marginal reaction, gross profit, energy and money, sustainability, and society and culture.

5. Fail-safe clauses

Every policy carries its own alarm: the moment an unintended negative consequence is confirmed, the policy is void, and the makers must return and debate it again in public.

6. The record is the audit

Every vote is measured against the context and published here before each election. The voters are the audit committee. No permission required.
