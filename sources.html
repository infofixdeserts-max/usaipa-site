Sources: Election Laws by State

Every ballot rule in the book, with the primary source to check it yourself.

The book's annex summarizes how a new party gets on the ballot in every state, how its candidates qualify, and what it takes to keep ballot status. Each entry was drawn from public summaries of state election statutes - chiefly Ballotpedia's compendium - plus each state's own election office. Below are the primary links for all fifty states.

Election laws change, secretaries of state reissue handbooks, and deadlines shift with every legislative session. Read your state's entry in the book, then verify it at the source, then call your county election office before you file anything.
